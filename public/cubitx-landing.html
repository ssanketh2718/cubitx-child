WHY CUBITX EXISTS

Knowing an answer is not the same as knowing how to think.

Children need opportunities to question, investigate, test ideas and reason through unfamiliar situations—not only recall information.

LEARNING SCIENCE

Active learning creates opportunities to think.

When learners engage with ideas, explain their reasoning and test their understanding, they do more than passively receive information.

METACOGNITION

Thinking about thinking matters.

Planning, monitoring and evaluating one’s approach can help learners become more aware of how they learn and where they need to adjust.

CRITICAL THINKING

Good judgement needs practice.

Evaluating evidence, considering alternatives and recognising uncertainty are habits that develop through meaningful opportunities to practise.

These are broad learning-science principles, not claims that one specific study proves CubitX improves outcomes. Product effectiveness must be evaluated through properly designed research.

Less passive consumption.
More active discovery.

CubitX turns these principles into child-friendly adventures where children can explore, make choices, test ideas and reflect on what they discover.
