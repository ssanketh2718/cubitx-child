WHY CUBITX EXISTS

Knowing an answer is not the same as knowing how to think.

Children need opportunities to question, investigate, test ideas and reason through unfamiliar situations—not only recall information.

ACTIVE PARTICIPATION

Learning involves doing.

Children need opportunities to engage with ideas, explain their reasoning and test their understanding—not only receive information.

REFLECTION

Notice how you are thinking.

Planning, monitoring and reconsidering an approach can help learners become more aware of how they are working through a challenge.

EVIDENCE AND JUDGEMENT

Good decisions need reasons.

Considering evidence, alternatives and uncertainty gives children opportunities to practise thoughtful judgement.

A FEW THINGS YOU MAY WONDER

Before you begin.

A little clarity for families exploring CubitX.

Which classes is CubitX designed for?

CubitX is designed for Classes 5–10. The learning pathways are grouped into Classes 5–7 and Classes 8–10, with challenges suited to each stage.

Can my child try CubitX without registering?

Yes. The introductory experience offers five free Day 1 adventures without registration. Guest activity evidence exists only during the current session; closing the session may lose that evidence.

Does CubitX give children marks or IQ scores?

No. CubitX is designed around evidence-linked observations of activity, not IQ scores, public rankings or fixed labels. One activity is not enough to make a stable judgement about a child.

What can parents see?

Parent Space is intended to show activities explored, responses or choices, observed thinking in action, what remains unknown and practical ideas for a parent-child conversation. Actual reports must be based only on recorded activity and validated observation rules.

How many children can one family account support?

One parent-owned family account can support up to three child profiles. Each child needs a separate paid and active entitlement for paid access.

Are paid plans and payments available now?

The prices shown are plan information only. Paid-plan selection and payment are not enabled yet. The five introductory adventures are free.

Does a subscription renew automatically?

No. The current pricing decision is manual renewal, with no automatic charges.

Can schools use CubitX?

Schools can contact CubitX to discuss a possible relationship. School access and staff permissions must be separately authorised; a school code alone does not grant access to a child's records.
