Privacy Policy for CubitX

Effective date: November 4, 2026

CubitX operates the platform to provide interactive thinking modules for students. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you use our application and Google Sign-In authentication.

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you sign in using Google, CubitX accesses basic profile information you have provided, including your name, email address, and profile image.

2. How We Use Information

We use the data we collect exclusively to provide you with the services you requested, such as authenticating your user account, maintaining session security, and personalizing your learning experience within CubitX. We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising or to train non-personalized AI/ML models.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal information or Google user data to third parties, except as required by law or to trusted infrastructure providers who assist us in operating our platform.

4. Data Protection and Security

Security procedures and encryption standards are in place to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and security of your personal data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for the length of time needed to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy. When your data is no longer required, we securely delete or destroy it. You may request account deletion and data removal at any time by contacting us.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at support@cubitx.in.
